L1109: Venus: hot and harsh
L1110: Flying to an inner Solar System planet like
L1111: Venus, at half the distance to the Sun as
L1112: compared to Mars, means that the effects of
L1113: solar illumination and ionising radiation on
L1114: the spacecraft are much higher. In fact, the
L1115: heating of the spacecraft is four times
L1116: greater at Venus than at Mars. To keep
L1117: Venus Express at safe temperatures, the
L1118: radiators on its surfaces have therefore
L1119: been increased in both area and efficiency.
L1120: The spacecraft’s protective jacket, known as
L1121: ‘multi-layer insulation’ or MLI, is composed
L1122: of 23 layers, packaged differently from
L1123: those on Mars Express. Moreover, the MLI on
L1124: Venus Express is gold instead of black in
L1125: colour, which allows it to reflect more
L1126: radiation. In general, Mars Express was
L1127: designed to keep warm, while Venus
L1128: Express needs to stay cool.
L1129: Plenty of electrical power
L1130: The Sun is twice as strong at Venus as on
L1131: Earth, so there is plentiful solar radiation to
L1132: power the spacecraft. Venus Express’s solar
L1133: arrays could therefore be made smaller
L1134: (almost half the size) than those on Mars
L1135: Express. The two symmetric solar arrays,
L1136: with a total area of about six square metres,
L1137: are based on a ‘triple-junction’ gallium
L1138: arsenide (GaAs) technology, rather than the
L1139: silicon-based technology used for Mars
L1140: Express. The solar cells, each composed of
L1141: four layers of gallium arsenide, are more
L1142: tolerant to high temperatures (up to around
L1143: 120ºC), as well as being able to exploit a
L1144: wider spectrum of solar radiation. The solar
L1145: cells are separated by aluminium strips that
L1146: help to reject heat. Altogether, these
L1147: aluminium strips account for half of the
L1148: solar array’s total surface area.
L1149: When the spacecraft is in shadow (eclipse)

L1151: capacity of the solar arrays, electrical power

L1153: which are charged by the solar generated
L1154: power.
