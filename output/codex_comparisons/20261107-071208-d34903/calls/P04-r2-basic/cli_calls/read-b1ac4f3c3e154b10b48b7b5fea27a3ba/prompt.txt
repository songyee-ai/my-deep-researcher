L24: Flying to an inner Solar System planet like Venus, at half the distance to the Sun as compared to Mars, meant that the effect of solar illumination and ionising radiation on the spacecraft was much higher. In fact, the heating of the spacecraft was four times greater at Venus than at Mars.
L25: To keep Venus Express within temperatures that were safe for the spacecraft, the radiators on the spacecraft surface were increased in area and efficiency. The spacecraft coating, called ‘multi-layer insulation’ or MLI, was composed of 23 layers, packaged differently from Mars Express.
